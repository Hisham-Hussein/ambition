# Ambition cheat sheet (for humans)

From Felix Rieseberg, "How to be more ambitious: A tutorial for humans with models" (13 Oct 2026).

**Say "ambition" to Claude** to get the full pass. Or ask for one lens directly:
`/ambition:altitude` · `/ambition:deepen` · `/ambition:crew` · `/ambition:judge`
(or just say "go up a level", "go deeper", "bring in other fields", "outside check").

## Before you hand over a task

| Lens | Ask yourself | Then ask Claude |
|---|---|---|
| **Altitude** | What do I want to happen *after* this? Will people meet about it? | "The real goal is X. Work toward that, and tell me what access you'd need to own it." |
| **Depth** | Would a top expert do it this way, with these tools, from this data? | "Answer the four depth questions first, then rewrite my prompt." |
| **Range** | What is this *for*, as verbs? Who else would I hire if the stakes were absurd? | "Assemble a crew with a red team. Methods, not personas." |
| **Judge** | How will we know it's right, without trusting the model's own say-so? | "Name an outside check and run it before you report." |

## The four depth questions
1. What would a top professional do that I'm not asking for?
2. What's the rawest material they'd start from?
3. Which methods and tools would they use?
4. What, other than us, would show it's right?

## Give Claude the means
A computer it can install things on, the raw data, and the access (connectors) to act and to
verify. Ambition without access is just a wish.

## For yourself (self-efficacy)
- **Specific and hard beats "do your best."** Name the number.
- **Add a deadline.** Audacious goal plus tight timeline.
- **Someone has done it.** Find the example; it moves your sense of what's possible.
- **Permission to fail.** Treat the attempt as an experiment; trying too hard makes it worse.
- **Your trust is narrower than your ability.** The sculptor can paint the ceiling.
- **Assume it's solvable.** The student who solved two open problems thought they were homework.
  Say the same to Claude when it gives up.

## End-of-session habit
"What would the ambitious version of today's work have been?" One line, then decide whether to do it.
If a new ambition technique worked, ask Claude to add it to the plugin's `lessons.md` under a new id.
